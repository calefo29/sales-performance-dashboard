# Sales Problem: synthetic dataset

Synthetic B2B SaaS sales data (Jul 2023 to Dec 2024), generated by `generate_data.py` (fixed seed, fully reproducible). Every company, person, ID and amount is random.

## Tables

| File | Rows | Grain |
|---|---:|---|
| Sales_Transactions.csv | 2,730 | One closed-won deal |
| Accounts.csv | 2,000 | One customer account |
| Sales_Reps.csv | 50 | One sales rep (10 managers have no ManagerID) |
| Date_Table.csv | 550 | One day; fiscal year starts July 1 (FY2025 = Jul 2024 to Jun 2025) |

Control total: BookingAmount sum = 116,845,073.

## Data quality issues kept on purpose

- The last fiscal quarter (FY2025 Q2) is truncated: volume drops in November and almost stops in December 2024.
- ChurnDate is only populated from December 2023 onward.
- About 20% of account names have trailing spaces.
- Contraction amounts are already negative.
- 100 accounts have no closed deal (untouched base).
- About 20% of deals are closed by a rep outside the account's region.
- QuarterlyQuota is a static attribute with no time dimension.
